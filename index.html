Prices: Sourced from Yahoo Finance via CORS proxy. Data is subject to a 15-minute exchange-mandated delay. Not suitable for live trading decisions — use SET.or.th or your broker for real-time quotes.

Buzz score: Counts ticker mentions across 3 Google News RSS pools (7-day window), weighted by recency (≤24h ×3, ≤72h ×2, older ×1), normalised 0–100. Proxy for discussion volume, not a direct post count.

Strategies: Rules-based generation from live buzz rankings and Yahoo Finance price data. Editorial judgment is required before acting on any strategy recommendation.
